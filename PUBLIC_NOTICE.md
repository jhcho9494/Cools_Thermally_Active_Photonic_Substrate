# Public Technical Disclosure and Collaboration Notice

## Purpose of this repository

This public repository introduces the architecture and industrial direction of the **Cools Thermally Active Photonic Substrate** platform.

The disclosed concept includes an alumina-based buried insulator, a high-thermal-conductivity handler, regionally and temporally controlled thermal pathways, optical/electrical co-integration, mid-infrared extensions and transferred III–V photonic functions.

The repository is intended to support:

- technical communication with photonic foundries and substrate manufacturers;
- joint simulation and prototype development;
- packaging and equipment collaboration;
- licensing and strategic partnership discussions;
- identification of potential automotive, data-center, sensing and optical-computing applications.

This repository intentionally presents the platform at architecture level. It does not disclose every implementation detail required for reproducible manufacturing.

Non-public know-how may include, without limitation:
